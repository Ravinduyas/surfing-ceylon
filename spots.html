Six waves within an hour of camp

The Breaks

The south coast packs sand-bottom beach breaks and coral reefs into one short stretch of road. We check the charts each evening and pick the wave that suits your level and the tide.

  1. 01

    Weligama Bay

    Our home break, and one of the kindest places anywhere to learn. A wide sandy bay where the swell arrives slow and crumbly, with long whitewater for first pop-ups and gentle green waves further out.

    Lazy Left

    Midigama's mellow left-hander. It peels slowly over a deep reef and gives you time to think, which makes it the classic step up from the bay and a favourite with longboarders.

    Kabalana

    Known as The Rock. An A-frame peak that breaks off a reef outcrop and picks up more swell than anywhere nearby. When the bay is flat, there is usually a wave here.

    Mirissa

    Hiriketiya

    A horseshoe bay further east, ringed with jungle. Soft waves in the middle for beginners and a left point along the rocks for everybody else. This is our day-trip wave.

When to come

The season

Sri Lanka has two surf coasts and they take turns. From November to April the south coast gets clean swell, light offshore mornings and dry weather. That is when the camp is open.

From May to October the monsoon swings round and the east coast around Arugam Bay comes alive. If your dates fall then, write to us anyway and we will point you in the right direction.
