From Colombo to the bay

Getting here

By car

Roughly 2.5 to 3 hours from the airport

  • The Southern Expressway runs most of the way
  • We can send a driver to meet your flight
  • Boards fit on the roof

By train

Colombo Fort to Weligama on the coast line

  • Slow, cheap and one of the prettiest rides on the island
  • Sit on the right-hand side for the sea
  • Camp is a short tuk-tuk from the station

By bus

Expressway buses to Matara, then local

  • The budget option
  • Change at Matara for Weligama
  • Tell us when you arrive and we will meet you
