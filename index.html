Ceylon

Ceylon is the old name for this island, and the waves are older still. We are a small surf camp a two-minute barefoot walk from the sand at Weligama, where the bay bends the Indian Ocean swell into long, friendly lines. Two coached sessions a day, video review in the shade, rice and curry at one long table, and nowhere you need to be after that.

Life at camp

Surf spots

Weligama Bay

Sand bottom, soft rollers and room for everybody. Where first waves happen.

Midigama

Lazy Left peels slow and long over the reef. Ten minutes by tuk-tuk.

Mirissa

A right-hand reef tucked under the headland. Faster, hollower, and best on a mid tide with a coach who knows the channel.
