Two minutes from the sand

The Camp

A small house under the palms in Weligama, built around two surf sessions, three good meals and one long table.

How it works

Slow mornings,
long table

We keep the camp small on purpose. A dozen guests, a handful of coaches, and a kitchen that cooks one thing well each night. You surf at dawn while the bay is glassy, eat like you earned it, sleep through the heat, and paddle out again when the light turns gold.

Nobody is rushed and nobody is left on the beach. If you want a rest day, the hammocks face west.

Rice & Curry

Lunch is the main event. Rice, dhal, three or four vegetable curries, coconut sambol, and fish when the boats come in. It is cooked in the house kitchen every day and eaten with your hands if you want to do it properly.

Breakfast is string hoppers, egg hoppers, fruit and a lot of tea. Dinner is lighter and changes with what the market had. Vegetarian and vegan plates are the easy option here, not the awkward one. Tell us about allergies when you write and the kitchen will plan around them.

King coconuts are cut to order, all day.
