Before you ask

Good to know

I have never surfed. Is that a problem?

No. About half our guests arrive as complete beginners. Weligama Bay has a sand bottom and slow, forgiving waves, and your first sessions are in waist-deep water with a coach beside you.

What should I pack?

Swimwear, a hat, light clothes, reef-safe sunscreen and a long-sleeved top for the evenings. We supply boards, rash vests and everything else you need in the water. Leave the wetsuit at home.

How do I get to camp from the airport?

Colombo's Bandaranaike airport is roughly two and a half to three hours away by car on the Southern Expressway. An airport pick-up is included in the week and two-week packages, and we can arrange one for shorter stays.

Do I need a visa?

Most visitors need an Electronic Travel Authorisation before they fly. Rules change, so check the official Sri Lankan immigration site for your passport well ahead of your trip.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, and most people do. One long dinner table tends to sort out the rest.

What if the waves are flat or too big?

That is what the tuk-tuks are for. There are six breaks within an hour, facing different directions, and one of them is nearly always working at a size that suits you.
